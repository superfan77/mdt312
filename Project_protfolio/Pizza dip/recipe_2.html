Recipe: Pizza Dip

Warm, cheesy, and bubbly pizza dip served with freshly baked pull-apart bread.

Ingredients

For the Pull-Apart Bread

  • 16.3 oz can Grands biscuit dough (8 count)
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 tsp grated parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning

For the Pizza Dip

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 tsp Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 cup marinara sauce (or pizza sauce)
  • 1 ½ cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 8 slices pepperoni, quartered (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Cut each biscuit in half, then roll each half into a smooth ball.
  3. Combine melted butter, parmesan cheese, and 1 teaspoon of Italian seasoning in a small bowl.
  4. Roll each dough ball in the seasoned butter, then arrange them around the outer edge of a greased skillet.
  5. In a separate bowl, blend cream cheese, ricotta cheese, and 2 teaspoons of Italian seasoning until smooth.
  6. Spread the cheese mixture evenly in the center of the skillet.
  7. Top with marinara sauce, shredded mozzarella cheese, and remaining 1 teaspoon of Italian seasoning. Add pepperoni slices on top.
  8. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until the biscuits are golden brown and the cheese is fully melted and bubbly.

Recipe Notes

Prep Time: 15 mins

Bake Time: 25-30 mins

Serving: 6-8 servings

Chef's Tips

Feel free to customize your toppings with sliced olives, bell peppers, cooked sausage, or fresh basil. Serve immediately while warm!
